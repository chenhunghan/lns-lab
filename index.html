Inside the microVM

lns runs AI agents, commands and OCI images in a local microVM. The VM has its own Linux kernel and filesystem, and the only doors into and out of it are the ones its document declares. This lab opens one up. Each chapter moves the camera to one mechanism and gives you something to try.

What you are looking at

Left slabYour Mac: the lns CLI, the lns-service background service, the project folder, a named volume, the artifact cache and the connector vault.
Glass boxThe microVM: guest kernel, the read-only image layers, the writable layer, the supervisor ring, and in the middle the workload: a little Claude Code whose face shows what happens to every request and file it touches.
The gateThe network boundary. Every outbound connection is matched against policy there: allowed, denied, or held while it asks you.
RightThe internet: destinations your workload might try to reach.

Accuracy

The field names, commands and behaviour follow lns 0.25 and its documentation. Every YAML shown here validates with lns artifact validate. Timings and sizes are shortened so you can watch them.
